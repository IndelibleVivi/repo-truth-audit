updated the CLI documentation and selected-delivery test. The original decision

candidate. The remaining account-partition defect is documented and unresolved.

installation, deployment or owner-client acceptance was made.

## Limits

Each condition ran once. Five of the eight intent subjects received these model
audits; the remaining fixtures have deterministic coverage only. There is no
baseline/candidate contrast, repeated trial, broad activation study, provider
attestation, arbitrary-repository proof or superiority claim. Findings about
unjustified accumulation and other unexercised intent categories remain specified
behavior, not independently established by this forward set. Conversation evidence
was synthetic and deliberately supplied; real private-history discovery was not
tested. The staged owner requests clarify permission and semantic scope, so this
does not establish autonomous resolution of every ambiguous implementation request.

The [product contract](product-spec.md), [evidence model](evidence-model.md) and
[current state](current-state.md) remain separate from this dated observation.
